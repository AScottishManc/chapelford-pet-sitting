The service gave me peace of mind on holiday regarding the care of my 3 cats. They eat different types of food and for health reasons it was important that they don't eat each other's food. Karen was able to supervise their meals and monitor their well-being. Daily texts and photos added to this reassurance. The service meant I didn't have to ask relatives to travel some distance and extra benefits included monitoring the house and giving the impression we were at home. Karen has a good relationship with the local vets and it was reassuring to know that should any of the cats have needed veterinary care, it would have been arranged straight away.

I would definitely recommend this service.

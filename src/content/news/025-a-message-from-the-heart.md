A message from the heart before we finish for our two week break 💗

A huge thank you to all my clients for giving me another incredibly busy and fun summer fully booked with visits. As a qualified cat specialist with International Cat Care I hoped my business would become mainly for cats and it has indeed done just that. Every day my heart is happy and full of gratitude for getting to do what I love the most - being with cats.

14 years now of doing this yet every night going to bed I am excited for the next day of visits and the fabulous kitties I shall be with. Every morning is just full of joy for the day ahead. Thank you to all of you 💗

It is now time for my husband Dave and I to enjoy our main two week holiday and boy we need it this year. You may recall we had the hard task of selling my Mum’s beautiful home and we were so consumed with all that last September. The September before was when we lost my beautiful Mum suddenly and unexpectedly. Last year we were distracted emptying her home so it’s no surprise that I only really started grieving for her this year 💔

Every single one of my clients has been supportive, caring and understanding. Every single one of you is helping me heal and so is doing all my fur filled visits - cats are such good therapy 😻. Truly a huge thank you. The grief is ever present and I miss my Mum terribly but I am getting stronger and I know eventually I will get to a place with that grief just beside me as I did when we lost my Dad 16 years ago… thank you 💗

This September gives us the opportunity to make it a happier one because although my Mums second anniversary is coming up, the heavy weight of it is being eased as we will be celebrating our 30th, yes our 30th wedding anniversary!!!! We have lovely things planned to do and a big celebration meal to share the occasion with people who mean so much to us 💗🥂🍾

We will also take the opportunity to enjoy every moment we can with our gorgeous kitty, our ginger ninja Errol. Most of you know he went very quiet very suddenly with us and the other cats. We monitor him closely every day and couldn’t quite put a finger on what was wrong but we were concerned it could be linked to his diabetes maybe. A vet check was in order and sadly after tests and scans we got the news he is in kidney failure and has masses on his liver and very enlarged lymph nodes indicating lymphoma. Our incredible vets have cared for Errol and us so well in the last 12 days and he also had a stay in the hospital for a couple of days to give 24/7 fluid therapy and that is what has bought us some good quality extra time together.

We know things will deteriorate quickly but how grateful we are that the timing is when we are off so we can be at his side and have this precious time all together as a family. Errol and his sister were born on my Mums birthday back in 2012 - it is a little poetic in a way that he may leave us to be with her around her anniversary 💗

So that’s it for now but I will share any updates on Errol and if we hear of other news like missing kitties while we are off 👍🏻

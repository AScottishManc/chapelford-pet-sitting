Following the sad news of the death of Mike Robinson from the Petsitters Alliance, of whom I had been a member of for some time, it was now necessary to find a new insurance provider for the business. Following recommendations from other pet sitters I know and research I did, I chose to go with Cliverton Insurance.

They were founded over forty years ago, and they have a working lifetime of experience in providing specialist insurance cover to animal related trades, rural and equestrian businesses, farms and smallholdings. Today they use that knowledge to maintain their position as the market leader in their chosen fields.

Their specialist cover, company structure, knowledge and experience were just some of the reasons I chose to insure with them. Not only does Cliverton Insurance give me peace of mind but it also ensures my clients can have peace of mind that I am insured with a market leading insurance specialist for what I do.

Please visit their website for more information and you can also see the various policies they offer. I always bring my policy details and insurance certificate with me when doing consultations for clients to see. [Cliverton](https://www.cliverton.co.uk/)

Starting off the New Year with some light reading, and continuing to further develop my knowledge around cat care, the arrival of the book the “ ISFM Guide to Feline Stress and Health” will continue to help me understand why cats can become stressed and distressed in many environments and this guide suggests how to improve welfare. Implementing some basic ideas, principles and tips can make a huge difference to cats and this knowledge is something I love to share with owners and cat owners in the community cat group I run.

The International Society of feline Medicine is the veterinary division within International Cat Care. It works with the veterinary profession across the world to provide cutting edge information for the veterinary care of cats and is recognised and respected for the standards it aspires to. I truly only like to learn from the best. More details on what the ISFM do can be viewed here [International Cat Care](https://icatcare.org/vets)

I also continue to support International Cat Care and their campaigns, to attend various webinars and read other published work that I feel is relevant to what I do and to keep myself up to date on many things cat related. The work International Cat Care continue to do is truly outstanding and I am proud to support them. More details of what they do are available here[International Cat Care](https://icatcare.org/)

I had been a member of the Petsitters Alliance for some time and my insurance was also with them. Mike Robinson, who ran the Petsitters Alliance sadly passed away this month and there was no one else who wanted to step in to run the company like he did. My sincerest thoughts were with his family, friends and colleagues after his sudden death. The Petsitters Alliance had been wonderful to be associated with over the years and Mike was always friendly and happy to help with any queries or questions.

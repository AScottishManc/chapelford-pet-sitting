It was brought to my attention that a number of people had issues contacting me using email. After some investigating, it appear that the issue lies with the provider.

To prevent any further problems, the email address that I can be contacted on has been changed to [chapelfordpetsittingservices@gmail.com](mailto:chapelfordpetsittingservices@gmail.com).

Additionally, the contact form on the website has been updated, and now includes Recaptcha. Unfortunately, we had to add this due to the number of spam emails being received through the website.

Thank you everyone who reported the issues with the website.

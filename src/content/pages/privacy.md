# General Data Protection Regulations

The EU General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR) require all companies to treat personal information collected or handled securely and maintain accurate records as to how this information is stored and used. This privacy notice provides you with details of how Chapelford Pet Sitting Services collects and processes your personal data through your use of the website www. chapelfordpetsittingservices.co.uk including any information you may provide in order to use my pet sitting or dog walking services during our consultation and when updates are given or changed in order for me to carry out the service effectively to give the best care to your pet.

If you are not happy with any aspect of how we collect and use your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection issues (www.ico.org.uk). I would be grateful if you would contact me first if you do have a complaint so that I can try to resolve it for you.

Chapelford Pet Sitting Services respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

# Cookies

This website uses cookies to track the activities and provide the best experience for our users. The service that we use to collect this is Google Analytics.

Google Analytics mainly uses first-party cookies to report user interaction on the website. These type of cookies store non-personal identifiable information.

We treat any information that we collect with cookies in the same respect that we treat other data provided by our customers, and only use this information as it was described within this policy.

Further information is available on the [BBC Website](http://www.bbc.co.uk/privacy/cookies/about/?source_url=/privacy/bbc-cookies-policy.shtml) about cookies and what they are.

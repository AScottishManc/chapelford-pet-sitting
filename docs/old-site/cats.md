# Cats

> Cats can be very territorial and they often react to changes in their environment and routine; therefore can get stressed quite easily. If they are able to stay in their own environment, they tend to be happier cats. ![Rosie the cat - one of my regular clients](images/cats.jpg)

As a passionate cat owner, I have studied cat behaviour and psychology; I just simply have a love of cats and everything about them just fascinates me.

The services below can easily be tailored to suit the needs of you and your cat(s). Just book a [free consultation](contact.php) with me to discuss your requirements.

I charge per visit *not* per animal. So if you have more than one pet, my services are even better value for you. If you have many pets that will require more than the allocated time per visit, then prices will be adjusted to suit your individual needs.

# Sitting Service

**Price:**

£9.00
per visit

- This visit will be for 30 minutes and can be carried out up to 3 times a day.
- Will feed your pet(s) either a regular or specialised diet.
- Water bowls will be filled with fresh water on each visit.
- Bowls and feeding utensils will be washed.
- Litter trays will be cleaned and topped up on each visit, and contents will be disposed in bags you provide into your outside wheelie bin. Additionally, I shall dispose of any hunting trophies that may be brought back, into your wheelie bin as well.
- Should soiling occur, the area will be cleaned with the products provided for me to use.
- Where possible, and if allowed by your cat, they shall be pampered and played with and given lots of fusses and attention. However, total respect is given to the cat who wishes to remain quiet and aloof and not be fussed. As many people say "The cat is the boss and we are just their humble servants."
- Your cat(s) will be encouraged to eat in your absence.
- I shall administer any oral medication that may be necessary that your pet may be on.
- If your cat is willing to pose for a photo, then a picture can be taken and emailed to you. For example, while you are on holiday and have access to check your emails, you can see how happy your pet is.
- Alternatively, you may prefer a simple text to simply reassure you all is well.

It is recommended your cat is kept indoors whilst in my care to ensure their health and safety. However should you wish for your cat to have free access to outside then I cannot be held liable for any disappearance, injury or death.
